6 JUNE 1944 · H-HOUR 06:30

MISSION 8 — DOG GREEN

Omaha Beach, Normandy · Sector Dog Green · Cpl. Walter Hale, US Army assault infantry, first wave

Operation Overlord is under way. Your landing craft is heading for the western end of Omaha Beach, below a bluff lined with concrete machine-gun positions. Get across the sand, breach the wire, and open the draw so the waves behind you can get off the beach.

  1. Survive the run-in and the ramp drop
  2. Move from obstacle to obstacle to the shingle bank
  3. Blow the wire with a Bangalore torpedo and open a path up the bluff
  4. Clear the strongpoint covering the draw
  5. Call destroyer fire on the bunkers and relay word from the Rangers at Pointe du Hoc
